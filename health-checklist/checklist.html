Indie Film Investment — Due Diligence Checklist

Scale: use this checklist in meetings; request the referenced documents where indicated.

Project basics

  • Project title & logline
  • Final shooting script (request)
  • Director’s statement (request)

Team & track record

  • Producer, director, writer, DP credits (verify)
  • Examples of prior completed work (watch)

Rights & IP

  • Proof of rights/option agreements for underlying IP (request)

Budget & financing

  • Full line‑item budget (request)
  • Sources & uses statement; committed financing list (request)

Schedule & deliverables

  • Production timeline & tranche milestones

Distribution & revenue

  • Sales/distribution plan; LOIs from sales agents/distributors (if any)
  • Pro forma waterfall and expected timelines

Legal & security

  • Draft investment/legal agreement (consult counsel)
  • Completion bond or insure-to-complete (if applicable)

Red flags

  • No verifiable credits; no budget; no distribution plan; missing legal docs
